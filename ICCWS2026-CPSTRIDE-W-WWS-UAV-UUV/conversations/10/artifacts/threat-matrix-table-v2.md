## Summary Statistics

**Total Elements Analyzed:** 53

- Processes: 7 (CPP1-CPP7)

- Cyber-Physical Flows: 1 (CPF7: Electricity)
- Physical Links: 6 (PL1-PL6)

**UAV/UUV Threats Identified:** Specific threat scenarios across reconnaissance, physical attack, contamination, communication disruption, and infrastructure destruction

**Highest-Impact Threat Vectors:**
1. **PS5 (Water Storage) + UAV:** Direct contamination of storage tanks (if accessible)
2. **UUV threats to PL1/PI1:** Contamination injection at source intake bypassing all treatment
3. **CPD13/CPF7 (Power Distribution):** Facility-wide operational disruption
4. **PTB1/PTB2 (Trust Boundaries) + UAV:** Physical breaches enabling access to critical infrastructure
5. **Physical Flows + UAV/UUV:** Contamination injection at multiple treatment stages

This comprehensive threat model demonstrates the critical importance of CPSTRIDE's expanded framework for addressing cyber-physical threats that purely cyber-focused methodologies would miss, particularly the emerging threat vectors from unmanned aerial and underwater systems.
